SYSTEM DESIGN · MOBILE ONLY (iOS + ANDROID) · NO WEBSITE

Tech stack, CAP & how data moves

The one decision everything hangs on: Voyagami is local-first. Travellers lose signal all the time: on planes, roaming abroad, in basements, on Ha Long boats. So the phone's own database is the first copy of the trip, and the cloud is where the group's copies meet. The rest of this page follows from that.

CAP THEOREM · WHAT WE PRIORITISE

Partitions are guaranteed, so we choose availability for trip content and consistency for permissions and money.

P · a given
A phone with no signal is a network partition. In travel that's normal, not an edge case. Any design that stops working offline fails the core use.
A · default (AP)
Plans, places, notes, journal, expenses and votes can always be read and written. Copies converge later through merge rules that never lose data.
C · where it matters (CP)
Who can edit, invites, account deletion and "marked as paid" need the server. Offline, those buttons say "needs signal" instead of guessing.

With a connection (the PACELC "else" case) we still pick low latency over strict consistency for trip content: your edit shows up on your screen instantly and reaches the crew in about a second. Nobody needs a global lock to move a café to 13:00.

Write path: moving a stop

  1. You drag Train Street to 13:00. SQLite updates and the screen changes instantly.
  2. The change joins the upload queue with a clock stamp and an idempotency key.
  3. When there's signal, the API checks your token, your role in this trip, and the rules (e.g. a stop can't end before it starts).
  4. One Postgres transaction writes the change and an outbox event.
  5. Sync streams the change to every member's phone, usually in under a second.
  6. The outbox goes to Pub/Sub: the Enricher re-runs travel times, the Notifier decides whether anyone needs a push.
  7. If the API rejects it (say your role changed), your phone rolls back and tells you why.

Privacy & security

  • Data stored in Mumbai; consent asked per permission, in line with India's DPDP Act, 2023.
  • Booking refs and inbox tokens encrypted with Cloud KMS.
  • Access checked twice: sync rules decide what reaches a phone, the API decides what's written.
  • Short-lived signed URLs for photos and tickets.
  • Export everything and delete account from Profile (E4).

"No website" still needs a domain

  • Invite links need iOS Universal Links and Android App Links, which means two small files on voyagami.app.
  • Someone without the app who taps an invite needs a one-screen page that sends them to the App Store or Play Store.
  • Privacy policy and terms URLs are required for store listing.
  • Recaps share as a video file, not a web page.

What will cost money first

  • Places, Routes, flight status, FX: paid per call. Cache by trip, batch route requests.
  • LLM calls: parse once per document, keep the result, never re-parse.
  • Cloud SQL HA is the one always-on bill; everything else scales to zero.
  • Gmail sync: yearly security assessment, which is why it's phase 3.

Free-first: start at ₹0, upgrade when it hurts

Every paid service above has a free stand-in for launch. The app talks to each one through a small adapter, so switching later means changing one adapter, not rewriting screens.
