RECOMMENDATIONS · DATA · ADS

Good picks, no scraping, honest money.

Every recommendation comes from a licensed source or from what Voyagami crews do in the app. Money comes from bookings people already want to make, never from paying to rank. Where our data is thin (events in Vietnam, say), the app says so instead of padding the list.

How a pick gets to the screen

1 · SOURCES
Open place data + partner APIs
Foursquare open places, OpenStreetMap, Wikivoyage, Google Places, Agoda, Booking.com, GetYourGuide, Klook, holiday calendars.
2 · OUR PLACE INDEX
One record per place
Loaded monthly into BigQuery, matched across sources, linked to the Google place ID. Split into a small file per city for the app and search.
3 · CANDIDATES
Only what fits this trip
In the trip's cities, open on its dates, within reach of the stay and each day's stops, inside the crew's budget.
4 · RANKING
Scored on fit, not fees
Crew style, saves and votes, distance, timing, weather, quality, variety. Commission is never an input.
5 · THE "WHY"
Two strongest reasons, in plain words
"Quiet before 9 am, which suits your slow mornings." Built from the signals, never invented.

A CATCH IN THE LOCKED STACK

Google's rules say Places results shown on a map must be on a Google map. We planned Mapbox.

Online maps → Google Maps SDK. Google lists mobile Maps SDK usage as unlimited; confirm it's $0 on your account before switching. Places data then sits on a Google map, as required.
Offline maps stay on Mapbox, but only with our open base data (Foursquare, OpenStreetMap) and what people typed in. No Google ratings, photos or hours offline.
Bonus: cheaper. Search hits our own place index first and calls Google only when someone opens a place. This is most of the "Places tuned" saving on the pricing spec.

What the ranking looks at

  • Fit to the crew's style from onboarding: food first, slow mornings, budget
  • The crew itself: saves, votes, what's already planned
  • Where and when: distance to the stay and that day's stops, opening hours on trip dates, weather, holidays and crowds
  • Quality: rating weighted by how many reviews it has
  • Variety: not five temples in a row
  • Never: commission, or whether the place pays us
  • Free vs Plus: free shows the top 5 per category; Plus shows the full crew-ranked list and smart filters (open on your days, fits a free slot, near your stay)

Day one, before we have any users

No crew signals yet, so picks lean on Wikivoyage's "must see" lists, Google ratings and the crew's style answers. The "saved by N crews" line stays hidden until it's true. Launch with hand-checked lists for the top 25 cities Indians travel to, at home and abroad, and widen from there.
The "why" sentence is filled from templates with real numbers. Gemini only rewrites it more naturally and is never allowed to add a fact.

Ads: yes, but only one kind

No ad network (no AdMob, no banners)
At Indian ad rates, one hotel booked through us earns about what thousands of banner views would. Ad SDKs also add tracking consent screens, slow the app and make Plus look like "pay to remove junk".
One sponsored card, done like a recommendation
  • Only in Explore and recommendation lists, at most one per list, never first
  • Must fit the trip (city, dates, a free slot in the plan)
  • Labelled SPONSORED, dashed border, "Why am I seeing this?"
  • At launch it's simply a bookable partner tour; sold direct to brands later
  • Shown on every plan, Plus included, so it must be useful: anyone can hide a sponsor for 30 days
Never
In the day plan, the money screens, the journal, live location, onboarding, the tour, notifications, or any error screen. No pop-ups, no video ads, no "watch an ad to unlock".

What bookings could earn at 50k users

Assumes 3 in 10 users plan a trip each month, an average stay booking of ₹10,000 at 5% commission and tours averaging ₹2,500 at 7%. Commission arrives after the stay is completed; cancelled stays pay nothing. Rates come from partner catalogues and third-party reviews, so treat them as estimates.

How bookings work, and when we get paid

Launch: we hand off, we don't sell
"Book on Agoda ↗" opens the partner's app or site with our partner ID and a trip tag. They take the payment, send the confirmation and handle refunds. We hold no money and run no support desk.
When commission is paid
Only when someone books through our link within the partner's cookie window (often ~30 days, shorter at some), and for stays only after the stay is completed. Cancelled bookings pay nothing. Payouts arrive monthly.
What earns nothing
Sights, restaurants and free events from Google, Wikivoyage or OpenStreetMap have no booking, so no commission. They're there to make the plan good, which keeps people coming back to the screens that do earn.
Closing the loop
The partner's confirmation email is forwarded or auto-imported like any booking, so it lands in the plan with a "Booked" tick. That's also how we see which picks actually turn into bookings.
Later: in-app checkout for tours only
Viator, GetYourGuide and Klook offer booking APIs where the partner stays the seller. That keeps people in the app, but we'd own more support. Worth it once tours sell well. Hotels stay as hand-offs.
