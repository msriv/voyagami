Voyagami, honestly reviewed

v1 looked good but cheated. It showed numbers, bookings, photos and forecasts without any way to explain how they got there. v2 adds the screens and flows behind every number on screen, adds onboarding and real group features, and fixes the problems below. v3 closes what was left: errors, offline, notifications, profile, a multi-trip home, dark mode, the recap and a budget step. The next step is testing with real groups, not more screens.

Build risks to know now

  • Gmail reading is a restricted Google permission: app verification plus a paid yearly security assessment. Ship forward-to-email and upload first.
  • Reel → places: Instagram has no open API for any reel. You'll get the caption and link preview at best, so expect misses and always show a pick list.
  • Maps, places, flight status, exchange rates are all paid per call. Cache per trip, or costs grow with every search.
  • Real-time sync: launch runs on Firestore's offline sync; Postgres + PowerSync is the path at scale. See Tech stack & CAP and Locked stack.
