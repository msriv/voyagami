BRAND REFRESH · V3 · JAPANESE CRAFT, FRENCH STATIONERY, PLAIN ENGLISH

voyagami

Voyage + origami: a trip folded into a keepsake. The name is French and Japanese, so the look is too: Japanese craft (washi paper, indigo and vermilion, seals, waves, folds) meets French travel stationery (an italic serif, airmail stripes, postmarks). Both live only in the look. Every word in the app is plain English.

VOICE RULES
No Japanese or French words anywhere in the app. Greetings, buttons, stamps and errors are plain, friendly English. The name tells the story; the paper, folds, seals and stripes carry the culture.
WHAT CHANGED FROM JAUNT
Cobalt → indigo · tangerine → vermilion · grey-white → washi paper · mint → matcha · dot logo → italic serif wordmark + paper-plane seal · waves and an airmail stripe on hero cards · postmarks and folded corners in the journal.
WHAT DIDN'T
Every layout, every flow, the type for UI and reading, spacing, corner radii and the stamp/ticket playfulness. Existing screens keep working as-is.
