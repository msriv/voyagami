System architecture
Local-first. Every phone keeps its trips in SQLite and works with no signal. Postgres in Mumbai is where the group's copies meet; the sync service keeps each trip's members in step. This is the design at scale: launch runs on Firestore's built-in offline sync (see Locked stack).
