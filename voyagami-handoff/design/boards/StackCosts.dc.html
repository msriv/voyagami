Free on purpose

Go API + workers on Cloud Run, Pub/Sub, Scheduler
Free tier covers launch; a few dollars a month at 10k users.
Booking-email intake
Cloudflare Email Routing + Workers for plans@ addresses. Also forwards hello@ to your Gmail.
"Best order" for a day
Worked out on the phone from distances; only the final route asks Mapbox. Saves a paid route-matrix bill.
Receipt & photo smarts
ML Kit on the phone reads receipts; photo matching uses EXIF. No server cost.
Analytics
Firebase Analytics + BigQuery export inside the free tier. Your home turf.
Crash reports
Sentry free plan until ~10k users, then Team at $26/mo.
Invite fallback page
Firebase Hosting serves the app-link files and the "get the app" page.
CI, infra, journal merging
GitHub Actions free minutes, Terraform, Yjs: all free.

WHERE THE MONEY GOES

At scale, about 60% of the bill is Google Places. Everything else is small.

  • Cut it without hurting quality: debounce search (300 ms, 3+ letters), fetch hours and photos only when someone opens a place, keep place IDs per trip so the same place isn't looked up twice by the crew.
  • Pays for itself: Yearly Plus (₹999) plus per-traveller Trip Passes cover the bill from roughly 3% on Plus and 1.5% buying a pass each month; tuning Places matters more than price. Scenarios are in the Plans & pricing spec.
  • Watch: set budget alerts on Google Cloud and Mapbox at 50% and 90% of each month's expected spend.

Assumptions behind the numbers

  • 40% of monthly users plan in a given month; each does ~8 place searches, opens ~7 places, sees ~3 photos.
  • ~15 route lookups per planner, half served from cache.
  • New sign-ups: 60% of MAU at launch, 30% at 10k, 20% at 50k; 40% choose phone; 80% of those are Indian numbers.
  • 30% of users use AI each month: 2 booking reads + 1 Ask.
  • Firestore: ~3,000 reads and ~300 writes per user per month.
  • Prices are list prices at the first paid tier; volume discounts not counted.
